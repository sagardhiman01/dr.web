What We Treat

Conditions We Treat

Our multidisciplinary team provides comprehensive evaluation, treatment, and rehabilitation for a wide spectrum of acute and chronic musculoskeletal, neurological, and pain-related conditions.

Spine & Joint Disorders

  • Neck & Back Pain, Sciatica
  • Slipped Disc & Spinal Arthritis
  • Knee, Hip, Ankle & Foot Pain
  • Shoulder, Elbow & Wrist Disorders

Sports & Trauma Rehab

  • Sports Injuries & Overuse
  • Post-operative Rehabilitation
  • Fractures & Ligament Tears
  • Tendon Repairs

Neurological & Chronic Pain

  • Stroke & Parkinson's Disease
  • Peripheral Neuropathies
  • Fibromyalgia & Myofascial Pain
  • Complex Regional Pain Syndrome (CRPS)
Technology & Modalities

Advanced Rehabilitation & Therapeutic Facilities

Our centre is equipped with a comprehensive range of modern physiotherapy and rehabilitation equipment, enabling us to provide personalized, evidence-based treatment for a wide variety of conditions.

We offer advanced therapeutic modalities including High-Intensity Laser Therapy, TENS, IFT, EMS, Therapeutic Ultrasound, SWD, and CPM Machines.

These advanced technologies are integrated with expert clinical assessment and individualized rehabilitation programmes designed by our multidisciplinary team.

Holistic Approach

Comprehensive Wellness & Lifestyle Rehabilitation

We believe that lasting pain relief and functional recovery require more than medical treatment alone. Our rehabilitation programme adopts a holistic approach, addressing the lifestyle and metabolic factors that influence health.

  • Personalized nutritional and diet counselling
  • Weight management & body composition programmes
  • Hormonal assessment and follow-up
  • Lifestyle modification and preventive health guidance

At our centre, healing is not just about relieving pain—it is about restoring health, rebuilding confidence, and empowering every individual to lead a healthier, more active life.
